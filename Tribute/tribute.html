The Life & Legacy

A Man Who Built a Nation

1923

Born in Singapore

Lee Kuan Yew was born on 16 September 1923 in Singapore, then a British colony. He was of Hakka Chinese descent and grew up speaking English, Malay, and later Mandarin.

1942

Japanese Occupation

The brutal Japanese occupation of Singapore during World War II profoundly shaped his worldview. He later said it taught him that strength and self-reliance were the only guarantees of survival.

1950

Cambridge Law Graduate

He graduated with double First Class Honours in Law from Cambridge University, returning to Singapore determined to fight for independence from British colonial rule.

1954

Founded the PAP

Lee co-founded the People's Action Party (PAP), which would go on to govern Singapore continuously — a record unmatched in democratic history.

1959

First Prime Minister

At just 35 years old, Lee became Singapore's first Prime Minister when it achieved self-governance from Britain. He would hold the office for 31 years.

1965

Independence — Against His Will

Singapore was expelled from Malaysia on 9 August 1965. In his famous televised address, Lee wept — he had never wanted separation. What followed was one of history's greatest development stories.

1970s

Economic Miracle

Under his leadership, Singapore transformed from a third-world port city with no natural resources into a global financial hub. GDP per capita rose from under $500 to over $12,000 by the time he stepped down.

1990

Steps Down as PM

He handed power to Goh Chok Tong but remained influential as Senior Minister and later Minister Mentor, continuing to shape Singapore's direction for another two decades.

2015

Passing of a Giant

Lee Kuan Yew passed away on 23 March 2015 at age 91. Over one million Singaporeans — nearly one in five — queued to pay their respects. World leaders from across the globe attended his state funeral.

"Even from my sick bed, even if you are going to lower me into the grave and I feel that something is going wrong, I will get up." — Lee Kuan Yew
